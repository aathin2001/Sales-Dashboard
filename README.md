# Sales Performance Dashboard

A dynamic Power BI dashboard built to track year-over-year sales performance and customer behavior.

## 📊 Overview

### Sales Dashboard
- Total Sales, Profit, and Quantity KPIs — current year vs previous year
- Monthly trend lines for each KPI, highlighting peak and low-performing months
- Product subcategory-level sales vs profit comparison (YoY)

### Customer Dashboard
- Total customers, total orders, and average sales per customer
- Customer distribution by number of orders
- Top 10 customers ranked by profit contribution

## 🛠️ Tools Used
- **Power BI** — dashboard development
- **DAX** — YoY calculations and custom measures
- **Power Query** — data transformation and cleaning
- **MySQL** — source data

Profit grew faster than total sales year-over-year, indicating improved margins rather than just higher sales volume.
